# Positioning & Messaging

## Category

**A thinking environment**: the first tool built for the **act** of thinking, not its **output**.

It is **not** a note-taking app, a mind map, a whiteboard, or a chatbot. When forced into an existing shelf, the least-bad comparison is *"a canvas for thinking problems through, with an AI that asks instead of answers"*.

## Positioning statement

> **For** founders, PMs, researchers, and strategists **whose real work is thinking a hard problem through**,
> **ThinkingCanvas is** a thinking environment
> **that** maps how your thinking moves and brings in AI at the right moment to extend it, never to replace it.
> **Unlike** chatbots, which hand you conclusions, and whiteboards, which just hold your sticky notes,
> **ThinkingCanvas** makes every AI contribution a question you answer and a choice you make, so **you own the conclusion.**

## Core narrative (the story arc)

1. **The tension:** AI can now produce any answer instantly, and more of our thinking is getting outsourced to it. The muscle that forms original conviction is weakening.
2. **The villain:** the *answer machine*. It isn't evil, just built to optimise for output, and it makes accepting easier than thinking.
3. **The shift:** the next frontier isn't a smarter oracle. It's a better **partnership**, an AI that makes *your* thinking go further.
4. **The product:** a canvas where your thoughts become a visible trail, and an AI that nudges one or two steps ahead, grounds before it asks, and never lets anything onto your canvas without your click.
5. **The payoff:** you reach a place you couldn't have reached alone, and *you* got there.

## Messaging pillars

| Pillar | Message | Proof points (✅ live · 🟠 not reachable yet) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. You stay the author** | Every AI idea arrives as a ghost. Nothing joins your canvas without your click. | Ghost nodes ✅ · per-node accept/reject ✅ · immutable north star ✅ · set aside and restore ✅ |
| **2. Questions, not answers** | The AI grounds, then asks. Every contribution ends in a question only you can answer. | Ghost pair grammar ✅ · Articulator gives 2–3 readings, never one ✅ |
| **3. It follows your trail** | It reads *how* your thinking moved, not just where it ended up, and stays 1–2 steps ahead. | Sequence as data ✅ · per-canvas memory across sessions ✅ · Expander 🟠 |
| **4. Help for the connection you can't name** | Draw the line first; understanding follows. | Question edge → Outer Subconscious ✅ · Relate edge → Articulator ✅ |
| **5. It knows when to stay quiet** | Help that respects your focus: silence by default, speaking when invited. | Observer speaks at Session Complete ✅ · consent timer, glow-first arrival, receptivity 🟠 |
| **6. It learns what you don't want** | Reject with a reason and it adapts, by listening to your refusals rather than profiling you. | Rejection Insights ✅ |
| **7. Your thinking has a home** | Canvases persist; open threads carry forward; revisit any past session. | Carry-forward ✅ · history view ✅ · SessionLanding ✅ |

> **Until the proactive AI is reachable (known gap #1), lead with pillars 1, 2, 4, 6, and 7.** Pillars 3 and 5 are true of the design. Only claim the ✅ proof points.

## Voice and tone

| Is | Isn't |
|---|---|
| **Thoughtful and calm**: like the product, it doesn't shout | Hype, urgency, "10x", "supercharge", exclamation marks |
| **Respectful of the reader's intelligence** | Condescending or over-explained |
| **Precise**: uses the product's vocabulary (see glossary) | Generic AI jargon ("leverage AI", "copilot") |
| **Warm and human**: a partner beside you | Cold and technical, or cutesy and anthropomorphic ("your AI buddy") |
| **Honest about restraint**: silence is a feature | Promising the AI will "do it for you" |

**Style notes**
- Short sentences. Room to breathe. Let one idea land at a time; that's the ghost-pair principle applied to copy.
- Show the gesture, not the architecture. "Draw a question line" beats "our multi-agent pipeline".
- Use the question as a device: end sections with a real question for the reader.
- Visual language: warm paper tones; solid means yours, dashed means pending; **amber means AI presence** and is never decoration.

## Claims policy

### ✅ You may claim
- AI suggestions never enter your canvas without your explicit acceptance.
- You can reject a suggestion with a reason, and the AI adapts.
- Drawing a question line brings a cross-domain perspective; drawing a relate line gets 2–3 articulations of the connection.
- Your goal is locked when you start, so drift is visible.
- Canvases persist across sessions; unresolved threads carry forward.
- An Observer reviews your whole canvas when you finish a session.
- You can start without signing up.
- AI-contributed nodes stay marked as AI, forever.

### ❌ Don't claim (not true yet, or never true)
| Claim | Why not |
|---|---|
| "AI expands your ideas as you write" / "suggests next steps automatically" | Expander unreachable (gap #1) |
| "Stress-tests your plan" | Stress-Tester unreachable |
| "Senses when you're converging" / "adapts to your thinking mode" | Attunement unreachable |
| "Asks permission before it interrupts" / "consent timer" | Intervention layer unreachable |
| "Pro plan" / "upgrade" / any price | No billing |
| "Interactive Observer structures" | Not built |
| "Collaborate with your team" | Single-user only |
| "Grounded in sources" / "cites research" | The Outer Subconscious is not retrieval-grounded |
| "Remembers how you think" / "cognitive profile" | v1.5, not built |
| "Makes you smarter", or any measured cognitive-benefit claim | No evidence yet. Claims about cognitive outcomes need substantiation. |
| "Private" / "we never train on your data" / compliance claims | No privacy policy or data-processing terms are defined in the repos. Get legal review. |
| "Writes your strategy / plan / essay" | Never. Contradicts the Prime Directive. |

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Why not just ask ChatGPT/Claude?" | "They're brilliant at answers. This is for when you need *your* answer, the one you can defend and own. A chat thread loses the structure of your thinking; a canvas keeps it, and the AI works from that structure." |
| "Isn't this just a mind map with AI?" | "A mind map records where you ended up. ThinkingCanvas records how you got there, and that trail is what lets the AI stay one or two steps ahead of you instead of somewhere random." |
| "The AI doesn't do much." | "By design it makes one small, well-placed move and hands the next one back to you. If it did more, you'd think less." *(Only honest once the proactive AI works; today, guide them to the Question and Relate pens.)* |
| "I just want the answer." | "Then a chatbot is the better tool for that moment, honestly. ThinkingCanvas is for the problems where being handed an answer doesn't help." |
| "Can my team use it?" | "Not yet. It's built for individual thinking first." |
| "Is my thinking private?" | Escalate. Don't improvise a privacy claim. |
